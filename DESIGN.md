## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of wind-tunnel engineering and artisanal atelier craftsmanship. Drawing inspiration from modern high-performance cycling and bespoke framebuilders, it communicates pure mechanical integrity, restrained luxury, and disciplined athleticism. 

The aesthetic is built on a foundation of **Technical Minimalism with Tactile Precision**. It repudiates the sensory overload of fluorescent race graphics, gamified badges, and visual clutter common in mass-market cycling platforms. Instead, it prioritizes:
- **Atmospheric Restraint:** Deep obsidian backdrops, muted matte surfaces, and razor-sharp linear geometry elevate carbon fiber textures and frame silhouettes into works of sculpture.
- **Engineering Discipline:** Typography, technical specifications, and micro-interactions evoke high-end digital cockpit readouts and luxury chronograph dials.
- **Conversion through Quiet Confidence:** Purchasing an elite machine or precision apparel requires trust. Layouts are spacious, legible, unhurried, and devoid of urgent promotional gimmicks.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an architectural dark-mode spectrum that prioritizes contrast ratios, material realism, and optical calm.

- **Primary (`#F1F5F9` - Pure Slate White):** The primary foreground token. Delivers crisp legibility against dark planes for headlines, key specifications, and active navigation indicators.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` - Precision Cobalt):** Used sparingly as an optical focal point for primary CTAs, interactive states, and telemetry data markers. It carries zero fluorescent glare, evoking anodized aluminum hardware.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` - Anodized Champagne / Titanium Warmth):** Reserved for bespoke tiers, limited frame runs, and specialized carbon layups. Applied with surgical restraint.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F1115` - Obsidian Monolith):** The base canvas. Grounded by deep graphite (`#16191F`) and matte slate tiers (`#1E222B`), bypassing sterile `#000000` for a richer, filmic depth.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#2A2E39` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Micro-fine boundaries that carve structured grids without interrupting visual flow.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Space Grotesk** for engineering-led displays with **Inter** for neutral, utilitarian body copy and telemetry readouts.

- **Display & Headlines:** Space Grotesk provides a technical cadence reminiscent of computational CAD blueprints and aerodynamic testing dashboards without sacrificing editorial poise. Headlines utilize tighter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor key imagery.
- **Body & Mechanics:** Inter functions unobtrusively for specs, geometry charts, and long-form editorial storytelling.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Technical specifications, component group tags, and frame geometries rely on `label-caps` and `label-tech`, utilizing generous tracking (`0.04em` to `0.14em`) in all-caps or tabular alignments to reflect instrumentation dials.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an architectural, asymmetric 12-column grid designed to let ultra-high-resolution product photography dominate the viewport.

- **Grid Discipline:** Desktop uses 12 columns with a maximum content constraint of `1440px` and default outer canvas margins of `4rem`. Tablet scales to 8 columns with `2rem` margins, while mobile relies on a 4-column system with `1.25rem` margins.
- **Negative Space Strategy:** Generous vertical rhythm separates engineering features, aerodynamic data modules, and build configurators. Content breathes like an architectural gallery space.
- **Divider Treatment:** Never use heavy separating bars. Structure sections with hair-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) that echo frame geometry drafting lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is governed by **Tonal Surface Stacking and Precision Edge Lighting**, completely avoiding diffuse drop shadows or blurred neomorphic illusions.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep Obsidian (`#0F1115`). The backdrop for full-bleed product imagery and editorial scrollytelling.
- **Layer 1 (Surface Containers):** Matte Graphite (`#16191F`). Used for specs panels, bike configurator trays, and technical comparison tables.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Panels & Flyouts):** Anodized Slate (`#1E222B`) with a crisp, low-contrast top rim border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Edge Highlighting:** Interactive surfaces capture a razor-thin internal edge sheen (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), mimicking light glancing off cold CNC-machined titanium.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is **Soft-Rigid (Level 1)**. 

To convey aerodynamic precision, component chassis, button containers, and dialog sheets use subtle `0.25rem` (4px) radii, transitioning to `0.5rem` (8px) only on large modals or media viewports. Round pills and bubbly shapes are strictly avoided; they erode the brand's mechanical, wind-tunnel-tested authority.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Purchase / Configure):** Solid Slate White (`#F1F5F9`) with Obsidian (`#0F1115`) text. Micro-radius (`4px`). On hover, shifts to cool titanium silver (`#E2E8F0`) with a subtle scale transition.
- **Secondary (Technical Specs / Compare):** Transparent background with a `1px` border of Slate Muted (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Text in Slate White. Hover transitions to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` surface fill.
- **Tertiary (Inline Action):** Understated text button accompanied by a monospace arrow glyph (`->`), animated with a `2px` horizontal drift on hover.

### Chips & Component Tags
- Compact height (`28px`), `4px` radius, styled with an obsidian interior (`#16191F`) and faint edge line (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`). 
- Typography adheres to `label-caps`. 
- No brightly colored badge pills; limited edition runs are flagged with an understated Champagne dot indicator (`#D4AF37`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background utilizes Graphite Base (`#16191F`) with a `1px` subtle outline (`#2A2E39`). 
- Focus state reveals a precise Cobalt accent border (`#2563EB`) without fuzzy outer glows. 
- Placeholder text sits at `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.4)`.

### Cards & Spec Trays
- Product listing cards feature edge-to-edge dark backgrounds with full-aspect frame photography. 
- Micro-dividers isolate technical data (e.g., Weight: 6.8kg | Groupset: Dura-Ace Di2 | Aero Drag: 204W).
- Information density is balanced by generous inner spacing (`space-lg`), letting component finish details stand out.

### Geometry & Build Customizer
- Custom frame-sizing interactive charts use mono-weight vector strokes, minimal coordinates, and clean radio pills with anodized Cobalt checkmarks.
